Following the official announcement of **Frontend Conference Fukuoka 2026**, we have launched our official website!

For detailed information about the event announcement, please refer to [this note article](https://note.com/sakupi01/n/n4e997b62c825) (in Japanese).

## Concept

### Thoroughly Engaging with the Future of Frontend

The frontend development landscape is rapidly evolving day by day.

At this conference, we value not only "sharing the present" but also "continuously exploring the future."

We aim to create a space where participants can thoroughly discuss what "must be talked about now" and engage in deep dialogue.

- **Capacity**: Approximately 200 attendees (expandable up to 300 depending on circumstances)

## Event Format

This conference will feature **no Lightning Talks (LT)**, with the following structure:

- **Topic-specific Sessions (40-45 minutes) + AMA (Ask Me Anything)**

We will invite experts from various technical fields to share their deep insights. Following each session, we will have an AMA period where attendees can engage in dialogue with speakers to deepen their understanding.
