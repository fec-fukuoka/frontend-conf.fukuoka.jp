## W3C Support and Philippe Le Hégaret's Participation Confirmed

We're honored to announce that **W3C (World Wide Web Consortium) Japan**, the international standards organization for Web technologies, has agreed to support this conference as a supporting organization.

- <img src="https://www.w3.org/assets/logos/w3c-2025/svg/w3c.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" width="35" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle; border-radius: 50%; margin: 0.3rem 0.5rem; background-color: white;" />[W3C](https://www.w3.org/)

W3C Japan shares our conference's [mission](https://frontend-conf.fukuoka.jp/2026/news/2026-01-13-announcement?hl=en#:~:text=Deeply%20Exploring%20the%20Future%20of%20Frontend) and will provide support through speaker participation and other content contributions.

We're thrilled to confirm that **[Philippe Le Hégaret](https://www.w3.org/People/LeHegaret/)**, a of W3C who currently serves as VP, Technical Strategy, will be speaking at the conference.

This is a rare opportunity to welcome PLH, who has been at the forefront of Web standardization, to Fukuoka. We look forward to seeing you there!
