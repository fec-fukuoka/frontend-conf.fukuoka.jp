## Introduction

Tickets for Frontend Conference Fukuoka 2026 go on sale today.

To mark the start of ticket sales, we've also published the timetable and talk lineup, and added our sponsor listings. We'll introduce all of these below.

With the event drawing near on Saturday, September 12, 2026, our preparations to welcome you are coming together. We hope you'll grab a ticket while you can, and we look forward to seeing you at the venue on the day.

## Ticket Sales

{/* TODO: リンク差し替え */}
Tickets are sold on [Peatix](https://peatix.com/event/5064536). Please purchase from the event page below.

- [Frontend Conference Fukuoka 2026 Tickets | Peatix](https://peatix.com/event/5064536)

The ticket types and prices are as follows.

| Ticket            | Price  | Notes                                                                             |
| ----------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| General Admission | ¥2,000 | For those attending in person                                                     |
| Fukuoka Resident  | ¥1,000 | General admission limited to those whose current address is in Fukuoka Prefecture |
| Student           | ¥0 | General admission for students                                                    |
| Networking Party  | ¥3,000 | Admission to the networking party after the main program (sold separately)        |

Please note the following when purchasing.

- General Admission, Fukuoka Resident, and Student tickets are **only for those attending in person**.
- The **Fukuoka Resident** ticket can only be purchased by those whose current address is in Fukuoka Prefecture. Please be ready to present proof of your current address at the entrance.
- The **Networking Party ticket is sold separately from the main program ticket**. The networking party ticket alone does not grant entry to the main program, so if you plan to attend both, please purchase it together with a General Admission or Fukuoka Resident ticket.
- Speakers and sponsors will receive separate guidance from the organizers. Please purchase through Peatix only if you need more than your allotted sponsor seats.
- Receipts can be issued and downloaded from Peatix.

## Timetable

To coincide with the start of ticket sales, we've published the day's timetable and talk lineup.

FECF 2026 is organized into **3 tracks with 18 sessions in total**, with 18 speakers across the open CFP, guest, and sponsor slots. Each track is hosted in a classroom named after one of our Platinum sponsors.

<TimetableEmbed lang="en" />

Under the theme "Deeply Exploring the Future of Frontend," each session runs 45–60 minutes — including an AMA — giving plenty of room for in-depth talks and discussion. You can also find each speaker's profile and talk details on the [Speakers](https://frontend-conf.fukuoka.jp/2026/speakers?hl=en) page.

Please note that the timetable and talk lineup are tentative and may change going forward.
